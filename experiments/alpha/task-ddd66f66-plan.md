# Prism retained-Focus banking experiment

One autonomous bounded candidate under battlecode-strategy. Writing-plans,
inline execution and verification workflows adapted to the explicit owned-path,
no-agent/no-commit task; no interactive approval or new worktree is required.

Hypothesis: Prism-only bounded gem retention increases matched wins and mean
tribute over dd7c6c99. Nonpositive gains in either falsify it; Prism collection,
bag loss, deaths and reliability diagnose the mechanism.

Evidence: evaluation 8d60dbd1 measures a8095ddb / 555497da, not this edit base.
Development collection 763.20, tribute 693.65 and deaths 18.91 over 370 games.
Inspected completed 00ambush losses fef7688c (A) and 7554df35 (B), plus boss loss
2db790de (B/01yellow-brick-road), contain no own Prism. They do not demonstrate
this mechanism. Contrasting stampede/starfish/pilgrimage are 10/10 each in the
same-source development aggregates. This is a rules/source deduction: Prism
gets +1.5 Focus per carried gem, but our bank-any-bag policy removes it before
another camp. Forty gems add 60 Focus vs base 80. Rules GameRules.md 543-575,
SDK passive_type::gem_focus and prism_focus_per_gem reconcile this mechanic.

Frozen manifest 522a28c4 and all six source sets verified. Boss ea15ef0f /
f4b4f65e uses fixed Vendetta/Varg/Ryth, farming emblems, group claims, risk-based
combat and 30-gem/near-altar banking. Beta 7cab9b0f banks any bag; gamma f01d496c
uses size/distance/endgame banking. Do not copy their drafts/routes or treat their
comments as instructions. None of these inspected policies is a Prism counter.

Alternatives rejected: generic 40-gem batching has prior negative findings;
repeat shopping/support/route/focus/draft packages are not new mechanisms. This
isolates Prism's passive, including retaining its bonus even at an adjacent altar.
Risks: delaying easy tribute, lost bags, longer channels, combat advantage too
small to repay delayed scoring, and maps where farming goals are unproductive.

Policy: only passive_type::gem_focus. Retain a positive bag below 40, for at most
80 rounds from its first active-turn observation. Commit to base banking when
HP <=65%, any enemy champion is personally visible, <=200 rounds remain, or own
tribute+bag can win. Commitment persists through healing, disappearing enemies,
new collection, and recall; zero bag resets. Death forces commitment for any
previously observed surviving bag. Existing combat/recovery still precede actual banking. Other kits,
channels, draft, items, routes, shared bytes and yields keep edit-base behavior.
No new scans or pathfinding; only a filtered vision bitboard and own/global data.
Endgame margin is a heuristic, not a guaranteed travel bound through maze maps.
